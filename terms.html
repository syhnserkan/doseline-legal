1. What Doseline Is

Doseline is a personal record-keeping app for people taking a prescribed GLP-1 medication. You record your own doses, weight, measurements, side effects and intake, and the app organises them, reminds you when a dose is due, estimates how much medication remains in your body from published figures, and produces a summary you can take to an appointment.

Everything it shows is derived from what you entered. It has no other source of information about you, and it cannot detect a mistake in what you typed.

2. Doseline Is Not Medical Advice, and Not a Medical Device

Doseline is a general wellness and record-keeping tool. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate or prevent any disease or condition, and nothing in it should be read as a clinical recommendation.

No part of the app tells you what to do with your treatment. It reports what you recorded and what follows arithmetically from it. Where it shows a schedule or a titration step, that is a restatement of the plan you entered, not a plan it is proposing. Where you record why a dose changed, that reason is the one you chose — the app never infers or guesses it.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your medication, your dose, your symptoms, and any decision about your treatment.

3. The Level Estimate Is a Model, Not a Measurement

The "in your system" figure and the curve behind it are calculated from published half-life values for the medication you selected and the doses you recorded. They are an estimate produced by a simplified model.

They are not a measurement of anything in your blood, they do not account for your individual metabolism, body composition, kidney or liver function, other medicines you take, or how a particular injection was absorbed, and they may differ substantially from reality. Do not use them to judge whether a dose "worked", whether it is safe to dose early or late, or whether any amount of medication is present. The app labels this estimate and its source on screen for the same reason.

4. Doseline Does Not Calculate Doses

Doseline does not work out a dose for you, and it does not convert a dose into syringe units. Where a record includes the units drawn from a vial, that number is one you typed in from your prescription label. The app stores it as you entered it, prints it on the export as you entered it, and never checks it against anything or produces one of its own.

What the app does count is inventory: how much medication is left in a pen or vial, in milligrams, from the doses you recorded. That is a total of what you entered, not an instruction about what to take next.

You are responsible for checking every figure before you draw a dose, against your prescription, the label on the container, and your prescriber's or pharmacist's instructions. If the app and your prescriber disagree, your prescriber is right.

5. Reminders Are Best-Effort

Dose reminders and refill alerts are ordinary local iOS notifications. They can be delayed, silenced or suppressed by Focus modes, Do Not Disturb, notification settings, low power mode, a device that is off, or your changing the app's permissions.

Do not rely on Doseline as the only thing standing between you and a missed or a double dose. A missed notification is not a reason to take an extra dose.
